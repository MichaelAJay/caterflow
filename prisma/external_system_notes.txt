A CompanyExternalSystemConnection is an image of an ExternalSystem record, with the only data difference
that the Connection record references the ExternalSystem and the Company.

When a Connection record is created, it is based entirely on the ExternalSystem record.

This extends to its assets,  but each of which maps to an external system's requirements,
with the exception that each asset has a 'status', and may have a value.

connection.assets = externalSystem.requirements.map(requirement => ({
    ...requirement,
    status: 'UNCONFIGURED'
}))

This raises a small issue, because secret assets now no longer have unique identifiers.
However, since they belong to the connection, the secret name could be something along the lines of:
`${connectionId}/${asset.type}`
